INDIA GATE

India Gate is famous as a war memorial that commemorates soldiers who died in World War I and other conflicts. It is also a popular public space, known for its iconic design, the Amar Jawan Jyoti.

QUTUB MINAR

The Qutub Minar is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Delhi, India, standing 72.5 meters tall and known as the tallest masonry tower in India.

HAWA MAHAL

The Hawa Mahal is a palace in the city of Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. Built from red and pink sandstone, it is on the edge of the City Palace, Jaipur, and extends to the Zenana, or women's chambers.
